Playroom: party games for tonight

Free party games on one phone, or open a room so every friend sees each card on their own phone.

Kitty Party games

  • Tambola Caller – calls the numbers from 1 to 90 so nobody needs paper slips.
  • Dumb Charades – act it out without speaking: Bollywood, everyday life or animals.
  • Antakshari Letters – a starting letter and a clock for the next song.
  • Kitty Questions – easy questions that start long conversations.
  • Truth or Dare – mild and friendly.

Couple games

  • Questions for Two – warm-up, closer and flirty levels.
  • This or That – quick choices that show how alike you are.
  • Date Night Dares – small things to do together.

Drinking games (18+)

  • Never Have I Ever
  • Most Likely To
  • Dare or Sip
  • Spin to Pick

House party games

  • Rapid Fire – name things against a 10 second clock.
  • Dumb Charades, Truth or Dare, Antakshari and Spin to Pick.
